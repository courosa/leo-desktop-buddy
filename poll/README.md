# Leo's Hair — the family ballot

Two ways to run the vote in the group chat:

**1. `leo-hair-ballot.jpg`** — send this image in iMessage. Eight numbered
options; kids reply with a number. Works on any device, no link needed.

**2. `leo-hair-poll.html`** — an interactive ballot. Tap a cut, tap
"Copy my vote", paste the result back into the thread. Published at
https://claude.ai/code/artifact/720673b8-18f8-43d2-aca4-07491fad836d
(private until shared from the page's share menu).

Options are cropped from the salon hairstyle analysis: 1–3 were its top
picks, 4–8 its "good options". The "not ideal" row was left off the ballot.
